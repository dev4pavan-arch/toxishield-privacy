ToxiShield AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

ToxiShield AI is an AI-powered content moderation application designed to analyze online comments and identify potentially toxic or harmful content.

2. Information We Process

When connected to supported social media platforms, ToxiShield AI may process publicly available comments and related information required for content moderation.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to analyze comments, identify potentially toxic content, and provide moderation recommendations such as allowing, reviewing, or escalating content.

4. Artificial Intelligence

ToxiShield AI uses machine learning models to analyze text. The system provides automated moderation assistance and its results may require human review.

5. Data Sharing

ToxiShield AI does not sell personal information. Information processed by the application is used only for the functionality of the moderation system and related development/testing purposes.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide, test, or improve the application's functionality.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of ToxiShield AI by contacting us using the contact information below.

8. Third-Party Platforms

ToxiShield AI may interact with third-party platforms such as Instagram through their official APIs. Use of those platforms is also subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.
